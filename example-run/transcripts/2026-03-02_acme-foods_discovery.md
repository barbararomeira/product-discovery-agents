**Dana Ruiz** [00:01:12] Thanks for making time. Before I show anything, tell me what a bad
day on the packaging line looks like for you.

**M. Hartley** [00:01:40] A bad day is when I find out at four in the afternoon that we are
two thousand units behind. By then there is nothing I can do. The shift is gone.

**Dana Ruiz** [00:02:05] How do you find out today?

**M. Hartley** [00:02:11] The supervisor walks the floor and counts. Then he tells me. If he
is in a meeting, I find out later.

**J. Silva** [00:03:02] We have the counters on the machines, but nobody looks at them during
the shift. We look the next morning, when the report comes out.

**Dana Ruiz** [00:04:20] So the data exists, it just arrives after it is useful.

**M. Hartley** [00:04:31] Exactly. I do not need more data. I need to be told when something
is going wrong, while it is going wrong. If your system can tap me on the shoulder at eleven
in the morning and say line three is drifting, that changes my day.

**Dana Ruiz** [00:05:15] That is on our roadmap for this quarter, real-time alerting.

**M. Hartley** [00:05:22] Then let me be blunt. Without that, this is another dashboard, and
I already have three dashboards nobody opens. With it, I can sell this internally. That is
the difference between us running a pilot and us not running a pilot.

**J. Silva** [00:07:44] Second thing, and this one is mine. When a line stops, the operator
picks a reason from a list. The list was written four years ago. Half the stops get logged as
"other". So my Pareto chart is a chart of the word "other".

**Dana Ruiz** [00:08:30] What would good look like?

**J. Silva** [00:08:38] Let me edit the list myself. Add reasons, retire reasons, group them.
I should not need a support ticket to change a dropdown.

**M. Hartley** [00:11:02] One more. We run four plants. I only care about mine, but my boss
will ask to see all four in one view. He will ask on day one.

**Dana Ruiz** [00:11:30] Understood. Multi-site is something we are exploring, not committed.

**M. Hartley** [00:11:41] Fine. It is not a condition for us. But he will ask.

**Dana Ruiz** [00:13:05] Last question. If we run a pilot, how would you decide it worked?

**M. Hartley** [00:13:20] If my supervisors act on something during the shift, at least twice
a week, without me telling them to. That is the whole test.
